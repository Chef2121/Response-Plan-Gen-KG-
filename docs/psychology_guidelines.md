# Psychology Guidelines for Traffic Management

## Core Principles

### Stress Reduction
- Use calm, reassuring language
- Provide clear, actionable information
- Acknowledge user concerns

### Cognitive Load Management
- Present information in digestible chunks
- Use familiar terminology
- Prioritize most important information first

### Trust Building
- Be transparent about limitations
- Provide evidence-based recommendations
- Maintain consistent messaging

### Emergency Communication
- Use urgent but not alarming tone
- Provide immediate actionable steps
- Follow up with detailed explanations

## Response Templates

### High Stress Situations
- "I understand this is concerning. Here's what we know..."
- "Your safety is our priority. Please follow these steps..."

### Routine Updates
- "Current traffic conditions show..."
- "Based on our analysis, we recommend..."

### Uncertainty Management
- "While we're still gathering information..."
- "The situation is developing, and we'll update you as soon as we know more..."
